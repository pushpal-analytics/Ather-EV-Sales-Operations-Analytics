EV manufacturers lose revenue daily to poor delivery visibility, untracked financing dependencies, and uneven showroom performance. Ather Energy was built to bring that chaos into a single, decision-ready view.

This project unifies sales, profitability, customer behavior, and delivery timelines into one automated dashboard — helping business leaders optimize pricing, streamline delivery SLAs, and maximize unit profitability.

## How It Works

The foundation is a **Star Schema** — one fact table (`Fact_Sales_Master`) linked to dimensions for product, customer, city, showroom, payment, and date. This structure keeps reporting fast, consistent, and scalable.

## Key Findings

- **Model dominance** — The 450X led all models with 3.58bn in revenue and 20,742 orders in the analyzed period.
- **Profit margin** — Achieved a 20.02% profit margin, with financing used in almost half of all purchases (47.9% penetration).
- **Financing dependency** — Finance and split-payment orders brought in close to 70% of total revenue, making financing partnerships critical.
- **Delivery bottlenecks** — 37.2% of orders were delayed, with delivery timelines varying more by showroom than by city.
